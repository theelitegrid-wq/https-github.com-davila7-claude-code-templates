# Top YouTube channels and how they make their videos

Compiled October 2026. Rankings move daily, so channels are grouped rather than ranked, and
subscriber counts are left out except where a recent source confirms them. Styles are general
observations; confirm the details for any channel you copy by running the `style-study` skill
on 5-10 of its videos.

**Biggest overall, for context:** MrBeast (~500M+ subscribers, mid-2026), T-Series (~313M) and
Cocomelon (~202M). Many other top-20 channels are record labels and TV networks (T-Series,
SET India, Zee Music, Goldmines, Sony SAB, Zee TV). They are left out of the list below
because their "style" is music videos and TV clips, which a creator can't copy.

## What this means for our channel

Our format sits with **Zack D. Films, Jake Fellman and What If** (bold above): 3D-animated
"what if" shorts. Patterns shared by the faceless winners:

1. **The hook is the question.** One short "What if...?" line, answered from the next second.
2. **One idea per video, under 60 seconds** for shorts; escalate step by step, then end on a
   twist or a question for the comments.
3. **Fast, consistent cuts.** A new shot every few seconds, always in the same transition style.
4. **A recognizable look:** the same characters, palette and caption style in every upload, so
   viewers know the channel from one frame.
5. **Research and accuracy** separate the channels that last (Kurzgesagt, LEMMiNO) from AI
   content farms, which YouTube now demonetizes as "mass-produced".
6. **Volume plus consistency:** the shorts winners post daily or near-daily in a single niche.
