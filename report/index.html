Niches

Top 10 niches, ranked for this channel

Ranked on viral potential, US audience, ad value, fit with our free 3D pipeline, and risk under YouTube's "mass-produced content" rules. Plain explainers underperformed in the study; the same topics work framed as a "What if" with stakes for the viewer.

Avoid: Reddit stories over gameplay (reused-content risk), ambient/satisfying loops (repetitive content), movie recaps (copyright claims), low-effort scary-fact lists.

Script formula

How every short is written

  1. Hook, 0–2 s: "What if you…?" in 10 words or fewer. Voice on frame 1.
  2. Answer, 2–5 s: the first consequence, no intro.
  3. Escalate: 4–6 beats, each more extreme, one fact per line.
  4. Twist: one surprising true detail near the end.
  5. Abrupt end: a punchline or question that loops back to the hook.
Editing & look

How every short is edited

  1. New shot every 2–3 s; mostly medium framing; one cut style.
  2. Same recognizable character every video; glowing x-ray cutaways.
  3. Word-by-word captions, current word highlighted, small and low.
  4. 2–3 big number pop-ups per video, gone in ~1.5 s.
  5. Whoosh on cuts, quiet music bed, loudness about −14 LUFS.

How long until the channel earns

Only engaged views count; replays don't. New channels' shorts usually get hundreds to a few thousand views each, so the target is reached through breakout shorts of 1M+ views, not upload volume alone.

Best case
~2–3 months

A 5M+ breakout in the first 6–8 weeks. Early access in 1–2 months; full monetization under the 10M rule if you apply before Feb 1, 2027.

Realistic
~4–8 months

Daily good shorts produce several 500K–2M hits by month 3–4. Early access in 2–4 months; full monetization likely under the 20M rule.

Common outcome
never

No breakout and views stay in the thousands. Fix the first 3 seconds using the retention graph, change topics, and keep iterating.

Pay once monetized: about $50–500 per million Shorts views, so 10M views is roughly $500–5,000, depending on audience country and niche.
