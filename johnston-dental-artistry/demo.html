The Practice

A new name for the West Seattle practice you already trust.

West Seattle Smile Co. has become Johnston Dental Artistry. The doors, the team, the free parking and the doctors are the same. The new name reflects the cosmetic work that has always been at the center of how Geoff and Niki practice: careful planning, natural-looking results, and plenty of time to talk it through.

Dr. Geoff Johnston

Co-Founder · Restorative & Implant Dentistry

Dr. Geoff Johnston

Geoff is the planner. He maps every restoration digitally before treatment begins, so patients see where they are headed and why. His focus is restorations that hold up to real life: implants, crowns and full-mouth rehabilitation that feel like your own teeth.

  • Dental implants & restoration
  • Full-mouth rehabilitation
  • Same-visit digital crowns
  • Bite & TMJ evaluation
“The best dentistry is the kind nobody notices. It just looks and feels like you.”
Dr. Niki Johnston

Co-Founder · Cosmetic & Family Dentistry

Dr. Niki Johnston

Niki leads the practice’s cosmetic work. She designs veneers, bonding and whitening plans around each patient’s face, lip line and personality, never a one-size template. Families also know her for gentle, patient care with nervous adults and first-time kids.

  • Porcelain veneers
  • Invisalign clear aligners
  • Cosmetic bonding & whitening
  • Family & preventive care
